Disruptive Mood Dysregulation Disorder (DMDD) F34.81

Severe recurrent temper outbursts manifested verbally and/or behaviorally that are grossly out of proportion. Outbursts occur on average >= 3 times per week, with persistently irritable or angry mood between outbursts. Symptoms present for >= 12 months in >= 2 settings, onset before age 10.

Diagnostic Criteria Checklist (Criteria A–J) Chronicity >= 12 months without > 3 month symptom-free gap; onset < age 10
A: Criterion A: Severe recurrent temper outbursts manifested verbally (e.g., verbal rages) and/or behaviorally (physical aggression) grossly out of proportion to situation.
B: Criterion B: Outbursts are inconsistent with developmental level.
C: Criterion C: Outbursts occur, on average, three or more times per week.
D: Criterion D: Mood between temper outbursts is persistently irritable or angry most of the day, nearly every day, observable by others.
E: Criterion E: Criteria A–D present for >= 12 months with no symptom-free period lasting >= 3 consecutive months.
F: Criterion F: Present in at least two of three settings (home, school, peers) and severe in at least one.
G_H: Criteria G & H: Age at onset is before 10 years; diagnosis should not be made for first time before age 6 or after age 18.
I_J: Criteria I & J: Never met full criteria for manic/hypomanic episode lasting > 1 day; cannot coexist with ODD, IED, or Bipolar Disorder.
