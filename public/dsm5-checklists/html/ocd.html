Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD) F42.2

Presence of obsessions, compulsions, or both. Obsessions/compulsions are time-consuming (> 1 hour per day) or cause clinically significant distress or impairment.

Obsessions (Defined by 1 and 2) Intrusive thoughts/urges and neutralization efforts
Obs1: Recurrent and persistent thoughts, urges, or images experienced as intrusive and unwanted, causing marked anxiety or distress.
Obs2: The individual attempts to ignore or suppress such thoughts, urges, or images, or to neutralize them with some other thought or action (by performing a compulsion).
Compulsions (Defined by 1 and 2) Repetitive behaviors/mental acts aimed at anxiety reduction
Comp1: Repetitive behaviors (e.g., hand washing, ordering, checking) or mental acts (e.g., praying, counting, repeating words silently) driven by obsession or rigid rules.
Comp2: Behaviors/mental acts aimed at preventing or reducing anxiety/distress, but are clearly excessive or not realistically connected to what they intend to prevent.
Criteria B, C & D: Time & Clinical Impact Confirm duration and exclusions
B: Criterion B: Obsessions or compulsions are time-consuming (e.g., take more than 1 hour per day) or cause clinically significant impairment.
C: Criterion C: Not attributable to physiological effects of a substance or medical condition.
