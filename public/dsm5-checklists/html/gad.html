Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD) F41.1

Excessive anxiety and worry occurring more days than not for at least 6 months, about a number of events or activities (work, school, health, finances). Difficult to control, associated with at least 3 of 6 physical/somatic symptoms (only 1 item required in children).

Criteria A & B: Pervasive Worry Both criteria required
A: Criterion A: Excessive anxiety and worry, occurring more days than not for at least 6 months, about a number of events or activities (such as work or school performance).
B: Criterion B: The individual finds it difficult to control the worry.
Criterion C: Associated Symptoms (Requires >= 3 of 6; only 1 in children) Present for more days than not for the past 6 months
C1: Restlessness or feeling keyed up or on edge.
C2: Being easily fatigued.
C3: Difficulty concentrating or mind going blank.
C4: Irritability.
C5: Muscle tension.
C6: Sleep disturbance (difficulty falling or staying asleep, or restless, unsatisfying sleep).
Criteria D, E & F: Impairment & Exclusions Confirm all criteria
D: Criterion D: Anxiety, worry, or physical symptoms cause clinically significant distress or impairment in social, occupational, or other functional domains.
E: Criterion E: Not attributable to physiological effects of a substance (caffeine, stimulant) or medical condition (hyperthyroidism).
F: Criterion F: Not better explained by another mental disorder (e.g., panic disorder, social phobia, OCD, PTSD).
