Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) F43.10 (Acute) / F43.12 (Chronic)

Exposure to threatened death, serious injury, or sexual violence. Symptoms persist for > 1 month and include Intrusion (>= 1), Avoidance (>= 1), Negative Alterations in Cognition/Mood (>= 2), and Marked Alterations in Arousal/Reactivity (>= 2).

Criterion A: Trauma Exposure (Requires >= 1) Direct experience, witnessing, learning of violent death of loved one, or repeated aversive exposure
A1: Direct experience of the traumatic event(s).
A2: Witnessing, in person, the traumatic event(s) as it occurred to others.
A3: Learning that the traumatic event occurred to a close family member or close friend (must have been violent or accidental).
A4: Experiencing repeated or extreme exposure to aversive details of traumatic events (first responders, child abuse investigators; not via media unless work-related).
Criterion B: Intrusion Symptoms (Requires >= 1) Persistent re-experiencing of the traumatic event
B1: Recurrent, involuntary, and intrusive distressing memories of the traumatic event(s).
B2: Recurrent distressing dreams in which the content/affect of the dream is related to the event(s).
B3: Dissociative reactions (e.g., flashbacks) where individual feels or acts as if event is recurring.
B4: Intense or prolonged psychological distress at exposure to internal or external reminders/cues.
B5: Marked physiological reactions to internal or external reminders (elevated HR, sweating, tremor).
Criterion C: Avoidance Symptoms (Requires >= 1) Persistent avoidance of distressing trauma-related stimuli
C1: Avoidance of or efforts to avoid distressing memories, thoughts, or feelings related to the event(s).
C2: Avoidance of or efforts to avoid external reminders (people, places, conversations, activities, objects) that arouse trauma memories.
Criterion D: Negative Alterations in Cognitions & Mood (Requires >= 2) Worsened mood and beliefs following the trauma
D1: Inability to remember an important aspect of the traumatic event (dissociative amnesia).
D2: Persistent, exaggerated negative beliefs about oneself, others, or the world ('I am bad', 'No one can be trusted', 'The world is dangerous').
D3: Persistent distorted cognitions about the cause/consequences of the event leading to self-blame or blaming others.
D4: Persistent negative emotional state (fear, horror, anger, guilt, or shame).
D5: Markedly diminished interest or participation in significant activities.
D6: Feelings of detachment or estrangement from others.
D7: Persistent inability to experience positive emotions (happiness, satisfaction, or loving feelings).
Criterion E: Alterations in Arousal & Reactivity (Requires >= 2) Trauma-related hyperarousal and reactivity
E1: Irritable behavior and angry outbursts (with little/no provocation), verbal or physical aggression.
E2: Reckless or self-destructive behavior.
E3: Hypervigilance (heightened watchfulness and scanning).
E4: Exaggerated startle response.
E5: Problems with concentration.
E6: Sleep disturbance (difficulty falling/staying asleep, restless sleep).
Criteria F, G & H: Duration & Impairment Duration > 1 month + clinical impairment
F: Criterion F: Duration of disturbance (Criteria B, C, D, and E) is more than 1 month.
G: Criterion G: Causes clinically significant distress or impairment in social, occupational, or other functional areas.
