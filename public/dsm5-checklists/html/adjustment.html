Adjustment Disorders F43.2x

Development of emotional or behavioral symptoms in response to an identifiable stressor occurring within 3 months. Marked distress out of proportion to stressor or significant functional impairment. Symptoms terminate within 6 months of stressor resolution.

Diagnostic Criteria Checklist (Criteria A–E) Onset within 3 months of stressor; does not exceed 6 months after termination
A: Criterion A: Development of emotional or behavioral symptoms in response to an identifiable stressor(s) occurring within 3 months of stressor onset.
B: Criterion B: Clinically significant: marked distress out of proportion to stressor, OR significant impairment in social/occupational functioning.
C: Criterion C: Does not meet criteria for another mental disorder and is not merely an exacerbation of a preexisting condition.
D: Criterion D: Symptoms do not represent normal bereavement.
E: Criterion E: Once the stressor (or consequences) has terminated, symptoms do not persist for more than an additional 6 months.
