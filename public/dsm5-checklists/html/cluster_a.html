Cluster A Personality Disorders (Paranoid, Schizoid, Schizotypal) F60.0 (Paranoid) / F60.1 (Schizoid) / F21 (Schizotypal)

Pervasive patterns of distrust, suspiciousness, social detachment, or perceptual eccentricities. Mnemonics: SUSPECT (Paranoid >= 4/7), DISTANT (Schizoid >= 4/7), ME PECULIAR (Schizotypal >= 5/9).

Paranoid Personality Disorder: SUSPECT (Requires >= 4 of 7) Pervasive distrust and suspiciousness where motives are interpreted as malevolent
S: Spousal infidelity suspected without justification.
U: Unforgiving (persistently bears grudges, unforgiving of insults/injuries).
S2: Suspicious of others (unjustified doubts about loyalty or trustworthiness of friends).
P: Perceives attacks on character/reputation not apparent to others and reacts angrily.
E: Enemy vs. friend (views relationships in terms of betrayal or hidden malevolence).
C: Confiding in others feared (reluctant to confide due to fear information will be used maliciously).
T: Threats perceived in benign events (reads hidden demeaning or threatening meanings into benign remarks).
Schizoid Personality Disorder: DISTANT (Requires >= 4 of 7) Pervasive pattern of detachment from social relationships and restricted emotional expression
D: Detached or flattened affect (shows emotional coldness, detachment, or flat affectivity).
I: Indifferent to the praise or criticism of others.
S: Sexual experiences of little interest (has little, if any, interest in sexual experiences with another person).
T: Tasks performed solitarily (almost always chooses solitary activities).
A: Absence of close friends or confidants other than first-degree relatives.
N: Neither desires nor enjoys close relationships, including being part of a family.
T2: Takes pleasure in few, if any, activities.
Schizotypal Personality Disorder: ME PECULIAR (Requires >= 5 of 9) Social deficits, cognitive/perceptual distortions, and eccentric behaviors
M: Magical thinking or odd beliefs influencing behavior (superstitiousness, belief in clairvoyance/telepathy).
E: Experiences unusual perceptions (bodily illusions, sensing presence of absent person).
P: Paranoid ideation or suspiciousness.
E2: Eccentric behavior or appearance (odd, eccentric, or peculiar mannerisms/dress).
C: Constricted or inappropriate affect.
U: Unusual (odd) thinking or speech (vague, circumstantial, metaphorical, overelaborate).
L: Lacks close friends or confidants other than first-degree relatives.
I: Ideas of reference (excluding delusions of reference; TV/events perceived as personal messages).
A: Anxiety in social situations (excessive social anxiety associated with paranoid fears rather than negative self-judgment).
