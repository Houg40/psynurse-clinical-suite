Cluster B Personality Disorders (Borderline, Antisocial, Histrionic, Narcissistic) F60.3 (Borderline) / F60.2 (Antisocial) / F60.4 (Histrionic) / F60.81 (Narcissistic)

Dramatic, emotional, or erratic traits with severe interpersonal relationship disruptions. Mnemonics: I DESPAIRR (Borderline >= 5/9), CORRUPT (Antisocial >= 3/7), PRAISE ME (Histrionic >= 5/8), SPEEECIAL (Narcissistic >= 5/9).

Borderline Personality Disorder: I DESPAIRR (Requires >= 5 of 9) Unstable sense of self, interpersonal relationships, and marked impulsivity
I: Identity disturbance: markedly and persistently unstable self-image or sense of self.
D: Disordered, unstable affect owing to a marked reactivity of mood (intense episodic dysphoria, irritability, anxiety lasting hours).
E: Emptiness: chronic feelings of emptiness.
S: Suicidal behavior: recurrent suicidal behavior, gestures, threats, or self-mutilating behavior.
P: Paranoid ideation: transient, stress-related paranoid ideation or severe dissociative symptoms.
A: Abandonment: frantic efforts to avoid real or imagined abandonment.
I2: Impulsivity in at least two areas that are potentially self-damaging (spending, sex, substance abuse, reckless driving, binge eating).
R: Rage: inappropriate, intense anger or difficulty controlling anger (frequent temper displays, constant anger, physical fights).
R2: Relationships: pattern of unstable and intense interpersonal relationships alternating between extremes of idealization and devaluation (splitting).
Antisocial Personality Disorder: CORRUPT (Requires >= 3 of 7; Age >= 18) Pervasive disregard for and violation of the rights of others since age 15
C: Conformity to law lacking (repeated acts that are grounds for arrest).
O: Obligations ignored (consistent irresponsibility, failure to sustain work or honor financial obligations).
R: Reckless disregard for safety of self or others.
R2: Remorse lacking (indifferent to or rationalizing having hurt, mistreated, or stolen from another).
U: Underhanded / deceitful (repeated lying, use of aliases, or conning others for personal profit or pleasure).
P: Planning insufficient (impulsivity or failure to plan ahead).
T: Temper / irritability and aggressiveness (repeated physical fights or assaults).
Histrionic Personality Disorder: PRAISE ME (Requires >= 5 of 8) Pervasive pattern of excessive emotionality and attention-seeking behavior
P: Provocative (or sexually seductive) behavior in interactions.
R: Relationships considered more intimate than they actually are.
A: Attention: uncomfortable in situations where he/she is not the center of attention.
I: Influenced easily (suggestible, easily influenced by others or circumstances).
S: Style of speech: excessively impressionistic and lacking in detail.
E: Emotions: rapidly shifting and shallow expression of emotions.
M: Made up: consistently uses physical appearance to draw attention to self.
E2: Exaggerated emotions (theatricality, exaggerated expression of emotion).
Narcissistic Personality Disorder: SPEEECIAL (Requires >= 5 of 9) Grandiosity, need for admiration, and lack of empathy
S: Special: believes he/she is 'special' and unique and can only be understood by high-status people.
P: Preoccupied with fantasies of unlimited success, power, brilliance, beauty, or ideal love.
E: Envious of others or believes that others are envious of him/her.
E2: Entitlement: unreasonable expectations of especially favorable treatment or compliance.
E3: Excessive admiration required.
C: Conceited: grandiose sense of self-importance (exaggerates achievements and talents).
I: Interpersonal exploitation: takes advantage of others to achieve his/her own ends.
A: Arrogant, haughty behaviors or attitudes.
L: Lacks empathy: unwilling to recognize or identify with the feelings and needs of others.
